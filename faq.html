Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?+

Most bundles are credited to your line within a few minutes of successful payment. If it takes longer than 15 minutes, please contact support with your reference number.

Which networks do you support?+

We currently support MTN, Telecel, and AirtelTigo bundles across Ghana.

How do I pay?+

You can pay using MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, or AirtelTigo Money directly at checkout.

What if my bundle doesn't arrive?+

Contact us with your payment reference and phone number, and we'll investigate and resolve it as quickly as possible.

Can I get a refund?+

If a bundle fails to deliver and we cannot resend it, we will issue a refund to the Mobile Money number used for payment.

Do bundles expire?+

Yes. Each bundle has a validity period shown at checkout (for example, 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days), matching the network's own bundle terms.
